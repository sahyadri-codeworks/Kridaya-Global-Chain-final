Kridaya Global Chain & Induszy:
Bridging Ground Operations with Digital Strategy

At Kridaya Global Chain, we manage the physical realities of SCM—negotiating with vendors, conducting on-site supplier audits, organizing quality inspections (TPI), and streamlining logistics. But in today's rapid industrial landscape, physical expertise needs digital acceleration.

That is why Induszy is the official Technology Partner of Kridaya. Induszy simplifies complex industrial strategy digitally. Together, we provide MSMEs and industrial operations with a seamless, unified SCM experience where ground-level execution is powered by digital intelligence.

Whether it is managing raw materials, structuring RFQs, optimizing old stock, or digitalizing vendor databases, Kridaya and Induszy collaborate at every step to maximize your cost savings and operational efficiency.

What This Partnership Delivers

How our technology-backed ground operations translate to success for your business.

Streamlined RFQs & Tenders

Compile complex bills of quantities (BOQ) and release RFQs digitally. Get rapid quote aggregations combined with ground-level verification of the bidding suppliers.

End-to-End Digitalization

Replace paper trails with structured, digitized workflows. Track supplier compliance, factory audit logs, and quality check status through cloud-based systems.

Scrap & Excess Stock Recovery

Leverage Induszy's structured marketplace categories (Scrap, RM, MRO, and Old Stock) combined with Kridaya's logistics coordination to liquidate idle assets effectively.

Forward & Reverse Auctions

Conduct seamless digital auctions. Execute transparent forward auctions for asset liquidation and reverse auctions for procurement to drive down raw material costs.

End-to-End Transaction Tracking

Achieve complete transparency with end-to-end milestone and document tracking for both buyer and seller transactions from order placement to final delivery.
